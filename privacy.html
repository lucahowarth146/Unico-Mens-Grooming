Your data

Privacy notice

Last updated 12 November 2026

This notice explains what Unico Men's Grooming does with your details when you book online. We keep it simple: we only use your details to manage your booking.

Who we are

Unico Men's Grooming, 172 Washway Rd, Sale M33 6RH, is responsible for your data. Contact us on 0161 385 8362 or in the shop.

What we collect

  • Your name, phone number and email address
  • Your booking: the service, barber, date and time
  • Any notes you add for your barber

Why we use it

To take and manage your booking: so your barber knows you're coming, so we can email you to confirm, accept or decline it, and so we can contact you if something changes. The legal basis is that you've asked us to book an appointment for you. We never sell your details or use them for marketing.

Who can see it

  • The shop owner, and the barber your booking is with
  • The companies that run the booking system for us: Supabase (stores bookings, on servers in London), Netlify (hosts the website) and Google (sends booking emails through Gmail)

These companies only handle your data to provide their service to us.

How long we keep it

Bookings are deleted automatically 12 months after the appointment date. If you'd like yours deleted sooner, just ask.

Cookies

We don't use tracking or advertising cookies. The map on our website is provided by Google Maps and the fonts by Google Fonts, so Google receives basic technical information (such as your IP address) when those load. Staff sign-ins store a login token in the staff member's own browser.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it. Contact us using the details above. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.
